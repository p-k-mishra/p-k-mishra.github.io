Research

Research interests

Nonlinear control under uncertainty and constraints, with emphasis on transparent analysis and implementable control structures.

Constrained nonlinear control

State, output, prescribed-performance and actuator constraints.

Approximation-free control

Unknown nonlinear dynamics without online neural or fuzzy approximation.

Adaptive & intelligent control

Adaptive, neural-network and learning-assisted nonlinear control.

Mathematical control theory

Stability, feasibility, invariance and control limitations.
