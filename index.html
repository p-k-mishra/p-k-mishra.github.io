Research interests

Control under uncertainty and constraints

The common theme in my work is the design of analytically tractable controllers for nonlinear systems when uncertainty and practical constraints cannot be ignored.

Constrained nonlinear control

State, output, prescribed-performance and actuator constraints.

Approximation-free control

Low-complexity control of unknown nonlinear dynamics without online function approximation.

Adaptive & intelligent control

Adaptive, neural-network and learning-assisted methods for uncertain nonlinear systems.

Mathematical control theory

Rigorous analysis of stability, feasibility, invariance and control limitations.
