Research

Control problems with constraints that actually matter.

My work moves from adaptive and neural control toward low-complexity approximation-free methods, while keeping feasibility and practical constraints central to the design.

01

Constrained nonlinear control

State, output, performance and actuator constraints with explicit attention to feasibility and invariance.

Approximation-free control

Adaptive & robust control

Intelligent & cyber-physical control
