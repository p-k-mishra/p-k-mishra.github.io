<section class="page-hero"><div class="shell"><div class="eyebrow">Research</div><h1>A coherent research trajectory.</h1><p class="page-intro">My research is centered on nonlinear systems with uncertainty and constraints. The recurring question is simple to state but difficult to solve: how much performance can we guarantee when the model is incomplete and the actuator is not unlimited?</p></div></section>
<section class="page-content"><div class="shell">
<article class="research-section" id="constraints"><div class="eyebrow">01 · Constraints</div><div><h2>Constrained nonlinear control</h2><p>Constraints are not treated as an afterthought. My work considers state constraints, prescribed performance envelopes and bounded control action as part of the controller design itself. A particular focus is the compatibility between desired performance and available actuation.</p><p>The recent line of work develops analytical feasibility conditions for simultaneous performance and input constraints, making the admissible initial-state region and control capability explicit rather than assumed.</p><div class="research-papers"><span class="pill">IEEE TAC · 2025</span><span class="pill">IEEE L-CSS · 2023</span><span class="pill">IEEE TCAS-II · 2022</span></div></div></article>
<article class="research-section" id="approximation-free"><div class="eyebrow">02 · Unknown dynamics</div><div><h2>Approximation-free control</h2><p>A common response to unknown nonlinear dynamics is to approximate them online using neural or fuzzy models. My recent research investigates when that machinery can be removed altogether.</p><p>The aim is not simplification for its own sake: it is to obtain controllers with lower structural complexity while retaining rigorous tracking and robustness properties under uncertainty, disturbance and constraints.</p><div class="research-papers"><span class="pill">IEEE TAC · 2025</span><span class="pill">IEEE TCAS-II · 2024</span><span class="pill">IEEE L-CSS · 2023</span></div></div></article>
<article class="research-section" id="adaptive"><div class="eyebrow">03 · Uncertainty</div><div><h2>Adaptive & robust nonlinear control</h2><p>My earlier work used adaptive backstepping, barrier Lyapunov functions, neural approximators, disturbance observers and related nonlinear-control tools for uncertain systems.</p><p>This line established the technical background for the current interest in reducing approximation burden while preserving constraint guarantees.</p><div class="research-papers"><span class="pill">IEEE TCYB · 2021</span><span class="pill">IEEE TCAS-II · 2022</span></div></div></article>
<article class="research-section" id="intelligent"><div class="eyebrow">04 · Intelligent systems</div><div><h2>Intelligent & cyber-physical control</h2><p>My early research also addressed vision-based path tracking, adaptive neural control, fuzzy processing and multisampling data transmission in automated guided vehicles.</p><p>This provides a practical counterpoint to the theoretical control work: uncertainty may arise not only from plant dynamics, but also from sensing, communication, image quality and network behavior.</p><div class="research-papers"><span class="pill">IEEE TETCI · 2019</span><span class="pill">Springer · 2019</span></div></div></article>
</div></section>
