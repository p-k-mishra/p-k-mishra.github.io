<section class="page-hero"><div class="container"><h1>Research</h1><p>My research is centered on nonlinear control under uncertainty and constraints, with emphasis on rigorous design and low-complexity implementation.</p></div></section>
<section class="page-body"><div class="container"><div class="research-block" id="constraints"><div class="number">01</div><div><h2>Constrained nonlinear control</h2><p>Control design for nonlinear systems subject to state, output, prescribed-performance and input constraints. A recurring objective is to make feasibility explicit rather than treating the constraints only as a controller-tuning requirement.</p><div class="paper-tags"><span>State constraints</span><span>Input constraints</span><span>Prescribed performance</span><span>Feasibility</span></div></div></div><div class="research-block" id="approximation-free"><div class="number">02</div><div><h2>Approximation-free control</h2><p>Control of uncertain or unknown nonlinear systems without online neural or fuzzy approximation of the unknown dynamics.
